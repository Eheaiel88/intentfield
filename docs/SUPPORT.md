# Support notes and macros

## Two surfaces, two accounts (by design)

IntentField runs in two places: the website (myintentfield.com, sign-in with
an IntentField account) and inside Whop (opened from the Whop sidebar, signed
in as the buyer's Whop account). These are **separate accounts with separate
saved work**, even when both use the same email address. Nothing links or
merges them, and support must never copy notes, grants or progress between
them by hand on the strength of a matching email.

Access lives where the purchase attaches: a website checkout unlocks the
website account the buyer was signed in to; every purchase also exists as a
Whop membership, so the buyer can open IntentField inside Whop and use a
separate workspace there. Progress made on one surface never appears on the
other.

### Macro: "I bought on the website — why is my Whop workspace empty?"

> IntentField inside Whop and IntentField on our website are two separate
> private workspaces. Your purchase unlocks both, but notes and lesson
> progress stay where you write them. If you started on the website, keep
> using myintentfield.com with the account you checked out with — your work
> is there. The Whop view is a fresh workspace you can use independently.

### Macro: "I bought on Whop — can I sign in on the website?"

> Purchases made on Whop unlock IntentField inside Whop: open it from your
> Whop sidebar and your workspace is there. A website account is a separate
> free account with its own workspace; it does not inherit Whop purchases.
> If you prefer working on the website, you can buy there with the website
> account you want to keep.

### Macro: "Can you merge my two accounts?"

> Our system keeps website and Whop workspaces fully separate for privacy
> and account-security reasons, so we're not able to merge them or move
> private notes between them. You can export your notes from Settings on
> either surface and keep both.

## What support can and cannot do

- Can: re-check a member's access ("recheck access" action, once Phase 3
  ships), confirm which surface a purchase unlocked, point the member to the
  right surface, process refunds per policy.
- Cannot: grant products manually as if paid (complimentary grants are a
  separate, clearly-labeled owner action), read private journal entries, or
  link/merge accounts by email.
